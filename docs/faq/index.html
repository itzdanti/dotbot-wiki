FAQ

Short answers to the questions people ask most.

Do I need to keep the bot online or host anything?

No. dat one ticketer is a hosted service. There's nothing for you to run or keep alive - you just invite the bot and it works.

Do I need any privileged intents?

No. It only reacts to the buttons, commands and menus it posts; it never reads ordinary chat messages.

How much does it cost?

Nothing to you - just invite the bot and use it.

Can I have more than one panel?

Yes - as many as you like, in different channels, each with its own types, style, channels and overrides. Use Duplicate to copy one.

Channels or threads?

Both. Set it server-wide and override per panel (or per nothing - channels and threads are a panel-level choice). Threads are private, auto-archive after 7 days, and are archived + locked on close instead of deleted.

How many ticket types per panel?

25 with Dropdown, 5 with Buttons (Discord's row limit).

Can users see each other's tickets?

No. Tickets are private - only the opener, staff/admin roles and anyone explicitly added can see them.

Are internal notes visible to the user?

No. Notes are stored on the ticket, posted to the log channel, and never shown to the opener or included in transcripts.

Where are transcripts stored?

They're kept by the service (so they show up in the Transcripts tab) and posted to your transcript or log channel as an HTML file. The opener can also get a DM copy.

Can I block someone from opening tickets?

Yes - use the Ticket blacklist (dashboard) or /blacklist add.

Can I change the wording and colours?

Yes, extensively - server-wide, per panel and (for some fields) per ticket type. See Customization.

Can I change the bot's name and avatar in my server?

Yes. The Bot profile (this server) card sets a nickname, avatar, banner and bio for your server only. See Server profile.

Are there ready-made panel templates?

Yes - Support, Sales, Report a user and Ban appeal, plus Blank. See Panel templates.

Why can't my staff see thread tickets?

Private threads only show up for their members and users with Manage Threads. The bot adds the opener but not your staff roles. Grant Manage Threads, Add User the staff member, or use channel tickets instead. See How a ticket opens.

Is there a list of all limits?

Yes - Limits & quotas covers panels, types, ticket fields and dashboard lists.

Where are the Terms and Privacy Policy?

Both are linked in the dashboard header and footer: /terms.html and /privacy.html. They are summarised on Terms & privacy.

Does it support other languages?

The UI text can be preset to English, Spanish, French, German or Portuguese, and every message is editable, so you can write it in any language.

Is it open source? Can I self-host it?

No. dat one ticketer is closed source and only runs as the hosted service - you use it by inviting the bot to your server.
