Customization

Messages

Welcome, system messages, transcript embed, pings and closing behaviour.

All of these live in the Message & colour customization card (server page or panel editor). Text fields accept placeholders.

Welcome message

The embed posted when a ticket opens.

FieldNotes
Colour (hex, blank = brand)Blank uses the brand colour, or the ticket's priority colour if the welcome colour isn't set.
TitleDefault Ticket #{number}.
MessageDefault Thanks for opening a ticket. A member of our team will be with you shortly.
FooterDefault {brand} - ticket #{number}.
Thumbnail URLSmall image, top-right.
Image URLLarge image.
checkbox Show "Opened by" lineAppends Opened by {openerMention} under the message.

The embed also always shows Priority, and adds Tags and Claimed by fields as they happen.

Buttons

Per action: label, emoji and style (Primary / Secondary / Success / Danger). See Buttons & priorities.

Priorities

Used by the Priority button. Colour is the embed colour when no welcome colour is set. See Buttons & priorities.

Mention pings

FieldNotes
checkbox Ping the openerAdds a mention of the opener to the welcome message.
Role IDs to ping (comma separated, blank = staff roles)Roles pinged on open. Blank pings your staff roles.

System messages

Short confirmations, each with its own text box:

FieldDefaultWhen
ClaimedTicket claimed by {mention}A staff member claims.
RenamedRenamed to {name}The ticket is renamed.
Member addedAdded {mention} to the ticketA user is added.
Member removedRemoved {mention} from the ticketA user is removed.
Ticket closedTicket #{number} closed. {note}On close - {note} is the transcript note.
Transcript savedA transcript with {count} messages was saved.Substituted into {note} above.

Transcript embed

Posted (with the HTML file) when a ticket closes.

FieldDefault
Colour (hex)Red (#ED4245) when blank.
Footer{brand}
TitleTicket #{number} closed
DescriptionOpened by {openerMention}\nClosed by {closerMention}\nMessages: {count}

Closing behaviour

CheckboxEffect
DM the opener a copy of the transcriptThe opener also receives the transcript embed + file by DM.
Delete the channel on close (otherwise rename it)Channels are deleted on close; otherwise renamed closed-<number>. Threads are always archived + locked.

{name} in the Renamed message and {userAvatar} in the welcome fields are the intended placeholders for those spots. {note} in Ticket closed is filled with the Transcript saved text.
